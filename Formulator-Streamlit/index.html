01  Top of the bottle

The oil phase

Emollients, butters, fatty alcohols and emulsifiers. Heated together, then poured into the water phase. Oil floats, so it sits on top.

    02  The body

    The water phase

    Water fills whatever the other ingredients leave, up to 100%. Humectants and water-soluble thickeners dissolve here.

      03  Bottom of the bottle

      Added cool

      Preservatives, pH adjusters and anything heat-sensitive go in last, once the batch has cooled. Small amounts, large consequences.

        04  Where the start comes from

        Read from five benchmarks

        The starting formula keeps every ingredient found in more than half of your benchmark products, then tops up the oil phase with the next most common oils. Hover a product to see what it shares with the bottle.
